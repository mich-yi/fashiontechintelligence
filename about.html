THE BEGINNING

Growing up watching my father build an apparel manufacturing business, I witnessed Korea's fashion industry evolve from OEM manufacturing to global sourcing, supply chain innovation, and today's technology-driven ecosystem.

That was where my own journey began. It inspired me to dedicate my career to the future of fashion.

FROM NEW YORK TO MIT

Over the years, that journey has taken me from fashion journalism in New York to MIT, where I earned a Master of Engineering in Logistics & Supply Chain Management, and later into product development, supply chain management, artificial intelligence, and enterprise transformation.

Through every stage of my career, I have come to believe that the future of fashion will be shaped by people who understand both creativity and technology.
WHY FASHION TECH INTELLIGENCE EXISTS

I created Fashion Tech Intelligence to share what I continue to learn. My goal is to make topics such as Artificial Intelligence, Digital Product Passport (DPP), digital product development, enterprise data, and intelligent supply chains easier to understand and more relevant to the fashion industry.

A CONTINUING CONVERSATION

I don't claim to have all the answers. I simply hope that the ideas, research, and experiences shared here will help fashion professionals navigate change with greater confidence and discover new opportunities for innovation.

If this blog helps even one person better understand the future of fashion, it will have achieved its purpose.
